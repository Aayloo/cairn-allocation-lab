    05 / RESEARCH Testable thesis

    What the study asks — and what would falsify it.

    Cairn is a process study. The aim is disciplined allocation, not a claim that a trend rule can predict every turning point.

    Read the five-minute case
    Research question

    Can a transparent policy plus a bounded trend overlay improve the decision process after costs and currency?

    The answer is judged against static SAA, a 60/40 proxy and an equal-weight basket. The primary evidence is repeatability and explainability, with performance as a secondary test.

    What would change the conclusion
    01

    Walk-forward results show no drawdown benefit after cost.

    02

    The apparent improvement disappears with a different FX or benchmark convention.

    03

    The trend rule changes too often to be practical or explainable.

    04

    Risk concentration grows beyond the mandate despite lower volatility.
